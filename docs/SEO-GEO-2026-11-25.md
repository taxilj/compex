# SEO and GEO launch checklist

## Implemented in the frontend

- Every public route has a unique title, description, canonical URL, and social card metadata.
- `robots.txt` allows public pages and blocks account, admin, portal, and utility routes.
- `sitemap.xml` lists canonical public pages only.
- The home page publishes factual `Organization`, `WebSite`, and visible `FAQPage` JSON-LD. It uses the approved Compex Solution name, GSTIN, address, telephone, and sales email only.
- Homepage questions explain the enquiry, BOM, and sourcing process in plain English for search users and AI answer engines.
- No schema claims product stock, price, delivery lead time, reviews, ratings, or customer outcomes that the project cannot prove.
- Product detail routes use the exact MPN in the title/canonical URL. When the read-only catalogue lookup is available, metadata also includes the verified manufacturer and product name.
- Known catalogue product pages now fetch their public record in the server wrapper and render the MPN, manufacturer, description and specifications in the initial HTML. Unknown MPNs retain the existing bounded client-side provider lookup instead of being fabricated for crawlers.
- Product detail pages publish factual `Product` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD using the MPN, manufacturer, category, public specifications, image and datasheet-safe fields only. Commercial fields such as price, stock, offers, ratings and availability remain absent.
- `sitemap-products.xml` emits canonical URLs for active catalogue MPNs and is advertised alongside the primary sitemap in `robots.txt`. If the backend is unavailable, it returns an empty product sitemap instead of inventing URLs or breaking the build.

## Required after deployment

1. Verify the production domain in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://<production-domain>/sitemap.xml` once. A sitemap helps discovery; it does not guarantee ranking or indexing.
3. Inspect the home page with Google Rich Results Test and fix only errors tied to the published page.
4. Ensure the backend catalog contains accurate, public-safe product records. A product URL cannot rank if the database has no active product record or the API remains unavailable.
5. Add original, owner-approved product, category, and technical guide content before trying to rank for competitive component keywords. Thin catalogue pages and copied manufacturer text will not create durable rankings.
5. Add the final custom domain to `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` before launch if the Vercel URL is not the intended canonical domain. Do not set a domain that is not live and verified.

## Not included

- Search Console ownership, sitemap submission, analytics, ads, backlinks, and business-profile management require owner-controlled accounts or an explicit separate authorization.
- Database, product-feed, ERPNext, and invoice settings are unchanged.
